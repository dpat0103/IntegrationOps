Operational case study

What happens when a payment service behaves incorrectly?

Run a repeatable sequence: normal responses, access denied, rate limiting, a slow response, wrong data, an outage, and recovery.

This runs 11 checks on your simulated Payment API. It illustrates failure classification and incident handling; it does not diagnose or repair a real payment provider.

Response times & monitoring details

Successful checks are the percentage of recorded checks that received an acceptable response, including slow responses. This measures check success, not continuous uptime.

A result becomes “Out of date” when no new check arrives within its expected interval plus a grace period. The previous result stays in the service history.
